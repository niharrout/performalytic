About the Role

We are looking for a B2B Marketing Executive to promote 4DAlert's enterprise software solutions, generate qualified business leads and expand our reach across global markets. The candidate will focus on targeted outreach, market research, digital marketing campaigns and engaging with potential clients to create business opportunities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Identify target companies, industries and potential customers for 4DAlert.
  • Research and connect with business and technology decision-makers.
  • Execute targeted email marketing, LinkedIn outreach and lead-generation campaigns.
  • Develop marketing content, product presentations and promotional materials.
  • Promote software solutions through digital marketing and outbound campaigns.
  • Manage prospect databases, follow-ups and lead nurturing activities.
  • Conduct market and competitor research to identify business opportunities.
  • Coordinate with sales and technical teams for product demos and prospect engagement.
  • Track campaign performance, lead responses and marketing results.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business Administration or a related field.
  • 1–3 years of experience in B2B marketing, IT marketing or software marketing.
  • Strong communication, content writing and research skills.
  • Experience in lead generation, email marketing and LinkedIn outreach.
  • Familiarity with CRM tools and digital marketing platforms.
  • A proactive, creative and target-oriented approach.

Preferred

  • Experience in SaaS, IT services or enterprise software marketing.
  • Familiarity with LinkedIn Sales Navigator, HubSpot or similar tools.
  • Understanding of B2B sales processes and Account-Based Marketing (ABM).
  • Interest in enterprise technology, data analytics and AI-based software solutions.

Key Objectives

  • Generate qualified leads and business opportunities.
  • Expand product awareness across target markets.
  • Engage potential customers and generate product demo opportunities.
  • Support the growth of 4DAlert through focused B2B marketing initiatives.

SEO, AEO & GEO Executive – Web Development

Marketing & Web
Full-Time Bhubaneswar 1 – 3 Years

Strengthen 4DAlert's online presence through SEO, AEO and GEO, improve rankings and organic traffic, optimize content for AI-powered search platforms and support the website with hands-on development and maintenance.

SEO AEO GEO GA4 Search Console SEMrush / Ahrefs HTML, CSS & JavaScript Schema Markup
View full job description

About the Role

We are looking for a skilled and result-oriented SEO Executive with knowledge of AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) to strengthen the online presence of 4DAlert. The primary focus will be improving search engine rankings, increasing organic traffic, optimizing content for AI-powered search platforms and generating potential business leads. The role will also involve website maintenance and development.

Key Responsibilities

SEO – Search Engine Optimization (Primary)

  • Develop and implement on-page, off-page and technical SEO strategies.
  • Conduct keyword research and competitor analysis.
  • Optimize website content, landing pages, meta titles and descriptions.
  • Improve Google rankings and organic website traffic.
  • Perform SEO audits and resolve technical issues.
  • Build quality backlinks and improve domain authority.

AEO & GEO Optimization

  • Optimize website content to appear in AI-generated answers and search results.
  • Improve content visibility across platforms such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity.
  • Implement structured data, schema markup and FAQ optimization.
  • Develop authoritative, relevant and answer-focused content.
  • Improve brand visibility and mentions across AI-powered search platforms.

Web Development (Secondary)

  • Maintain and update the 4DAlert website.
  • Develop and optimize website pages and landing pages.
  • Improve website speed, responsiveness and user experience.
  • Implement SEO-friendly website structures.
  • Handle basic website development and technical issues.

Required Skills

  • Bachelor's degree in IT, Computer Science, Marketing or a related field.
  • 1–3 years of experience in SEO and web development.
  • Strong knowledge of SEO, AEO and GEO concepts.
  • Experience with Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console.
  • Familiarity with SEMrush, Ahrefs or similar SEO tools.
  • Basic knowledge of HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Understanding of schema markup, structured data and content optimization.

Preferred Skills

  • Experience in B2B or SaaS product marketing.
  • Knowledge of WordPress or other CMS platforms.
  • Understanding of AI-powered search and content optimization.
  • Experience with website performance and conversion optimization.

Key Performance Areas

  • Improved organic search rankings and website traffic.
  • Increased visibility in AI-powered search results.
  • Improved keyword rankings and quality backlinks.
  • Better website performance and user experience.
  • Increased organic enquiries and business leads.
