Security Features

Built for enterprise security

Every layer of our platform is designed with security in mind — from deployment to data handling.

VPC Deployment

Deploy inside your Virtual Private Cloud on AWS, GCP, or Azure. Your data never leaves your security perimeter.

Data Encryption

AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. All data is encrypted before it touches any storage layer.

Audit Logging

Complete audit trail of all data access, transformations, and configuration changes. Ready for compliance reviews.

Role-Based Access Control

Granular permissions with SSO integration. Control who sees what across your entire data operations team.

Single-tenant deployments with dedicated infrastructure. No shared resources, no cross-tenant data exposure risk.

Choose where your data lives. Deploy to specific AWS, GCP, or Azure regions to meet data sovereignty requirements.
